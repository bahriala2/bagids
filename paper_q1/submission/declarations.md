## Data availability statement

The CICIDS2017 dataset used in this study is publicly available from the
Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity, University of New Brunswick
(https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html). The three neural detectors
were trained and published in our prior study (IEEE AMCAI 2023,
doi:10.1109/AMCAI59331.2023.10431511), and the exact preprocessing protocol
is fully specified in the manuscript (Section 4.2.1). The complete source
code, including the agents, the Hyperledger Fabric chaincode, the supervision
console, and every measurement script behind the reported tables and figures,
is held in a version-controlled repository and will be made publicly
available upon acceptance.
